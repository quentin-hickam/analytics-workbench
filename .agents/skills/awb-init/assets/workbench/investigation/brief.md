# Investigation brief

## Business question

State the question this investigation will answer.

## Population and scope

Describe the population, time period, inclusions, exclusions, and other boundaries. Treat these as settings unless they change the meaning of a shared term.

## Purpose

Record the decision this analysis will support, or state that its purpose is exploratory.

## Audience

Record who will read the package, what they already know, the decision they own, and the terms they use. Leave it blank until the user names the audience; packaging asks for it when it is missing.

Once the audience is named, always record precision and clock format: the user's choices, otherwise audience defaults. Unless the audience is recorded as technical, use whole numbers for large values, or one decimal with a scale word (8.6 billion), one decimal for shares and rates, and the audience's usual clock format.

Record, when stated, the methods note length as `Methods note length: about <n> words` on its own line (one page is about 500), which `check-draft` compares with the note; the deck length (such as about 8 slides); and the storyline checkpoint mode: **preview-and-wait**, headlines before drafting, or **draft-then-review**, the full draft with provisional headlines. Later packages reuse these choices; a request naming a mode overrides the recorded one, and one supplying the storyline or requesting the full draft gets draft-then-review.

## Usefulness criteria

Describe what would make the answer useful to its intended audience.

## Material unknowns

Open questions live in the question ledger, `questions.md`, kept with `python3 src/awb.py questions`; list none here. An open question need not block useful exploration.

## Starting method, settings, and package format

Record the inherited method, settings, and package format when this investigation is related to an earlier one. They are a starting configuration, not evidence about this population. The settings themselves live in the investigation's settings file.

## Local terms or departures

Record only deliberate differences from `foundation/glossary.md`, with the reason and analytical consequence. Shared definitions remain in the foundation glossary.
