# Investigation brief

## Business question

State the question that this investigation will answer.

## Population and scope

Describe the population, time period, inclusions, exclusions, and other boundaries. Treat these as settings unless they change the meaning of a shared term.

## Purpose

Record the decision that this analysis will support, or state that its purpose is exploratory.

## Audience

Record who will read the package, what they already know, the decision that they own, and the terms that they use. Leave this section blank until the user names the audience. Packaging asks for the audience when it is missing.

## Usefulness criteria

Describe what makes the answer useful to its intended audience.

## Material unknowns

List unresolved facts or decisions that it is better to keep visible. An unknown does not have to block useful exploration.

## Starting method, settings, and package format

When this investigation is related to an earlier one, record the inherited method, settings, and package format. They are a starting configuration, not evidence about this population. The settings themselves are in the settings file of the investigation.

## Local terms or departures

Record only deliberate differences from `foundation/glossary.md`, with the reason and the analytical consequence. Shared definitions stay in the foundation glossary.
