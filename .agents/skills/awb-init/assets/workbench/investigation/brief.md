# Investigation brief

## Business question

State the question this investigation will answer.

## Population and scope

Describe the population, time period, inclusions, exclusions, and other boundaries. Treat these as settings unless they change the meaning of a shared term.

## Purpose

Record the decision this analysis will support, or state that its purpose is exploratory.

## Usefulness criteria

Describe what would make the answer useful to its intended audience.

## Material unknowns

List unresolved facts or decisions that should remain visible. An unknown need not block useful exploration.

## Starting method and settings

Record inherited methods or parameters when this investigation is related to an earlier one. They are a starting configuration, not evidence about this population.

## Local terms or departures

Record only deliberate differences from `foundation/glossary.md`, with the reason and analytical consequence. Shared definitions remain in the foundation glossary.
