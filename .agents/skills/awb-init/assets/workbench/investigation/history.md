# Investigation history

Keep a chronological record of meaningful findings, analytical decisions, caveats, superseded conclusions, and data limitations that affected this investigation. Retain a methodological mistake when it changed a finding or explains why an earlier conclusion was wrong, recorded as what was done, what it affected, and the corrected approach. Exclude routine debugging, coding mistakes, and abandoned execution attempts that changed no understanding.
