# Investigation history

Keep a chronological record of meaningful findings, analytical decisions, caveats, superseded conclusions, and data limitations that affected this investigation. Keep a methodological mistake when it changed a finding or when it explains why an earlier conclusion was wrong. Record it as what was done, what it affected, and the corrected approach. Do not include routine debugging, coding mistakes, and abandoned execution attempts that changed no understanding.
