## Executive context

State the answer and why it matters. Keep the full reasoning and qualifications in the sections below.

## Scope and definitions

Describe included and excluded populations, periods, local terminology, and material assumptions. Explain any departure from the shared glossary.

## Sources and data quality

Identify the inputs used and summarize their fitness for this analysis. Include source limitations, known data errors, and corrections that affect interpretation.

## Method and settings

Explain the analytical approach and the settings needed to understand the results. Distinguish canonical preparation from investigation-specific filters or assumptions. State the validation checks performed on the results presented.

## Findings

Present the findings with supporting evidence. Include relevant results that complicate or contradict the main interpretation.

## Implications

Connect the findings to the intended decision or exploratory purpose. Keep recommendations within the evidence.

## Limitations and unresolved caveats

List interpretation limits and unresolved issues. Put a clear caveat beside every place a conclusion still flagged for revalidation appears: the claim in the text, a figure's caption and alt text, and a dataset's description.

## Changes since previous release

For later releases, describe material changes to scope, inputs, method, findings, caveats, or selected datasets. For the first release, state that there is no previous release.

## Supporting datasets

List the audience-facing datasets selected for this package, or state **None**. Describe their role and any caveat on a dataset or column without duplicating manifest provenance.
