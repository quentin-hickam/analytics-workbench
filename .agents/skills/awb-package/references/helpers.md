# Diagnosing a helper's rows

`awb-init`'s `scripts/install_helpers.py` installs `src/packaging/draft.py` and restores it when missing. Call the project's copy, customized or not; open its source only to adapt or debug it.

- `findings` rows (`check_findings(path, *, names=(), roots=())`): `line`, `kind` (`code`, `path`, `file`, `identifier`, `sha`, or `name`), and `text`. HTML comments and Markdown link targets are skipped.
- A `name` row's `text` is a name from these sources: the investigation and package names; the view and table names created or read in `foundation/views/*.sql` and in the views the investigation's evidence cites, without CTE names or table functions; the settings keys of `settings.toml` and of the settings files and resolved settings that the investigation's evidence records, as top-level keys and the dotted path of every nested key (a nested key's bare name is usually a plain word, so it is left out); and the result IDs of `investigations/<investigation>/evidence/*.json` and the evidence links in its brief, state, and history. A name of letters only is never in the set, since it collides with ordinary words. `check-draft` passes those plain-word names as `roots` instead: a slash token with any recorded name as a segment, such as `orders/region`, is a `path` row, while a slash between two other plain words, such as yes/no, is prose.
- `charts` rows (`check_charts(findings_path, charts_dir)`): `chart` and `problem`, one per specification numbered out of order, specification without a file, file without a specification, non-chart file in `charts/`, or file without data rows.
- `numbers` rows (`check_numbers(path, values, *, allowed=())`): `file`, `line`, and `text`, the number as written with its unit, such as `21.3%`, `4.2M`, or `1.5e6`; a currency sign is left out. The values are each represented result's `results/<id>.csv` cells, the numbers in its `results/<id>.profile.json`, and its evidence's check details and settings, as magnitudes, so a sign carried by words such as "fell" still traces. A cell that is a number whole is that number; any other cell, check detail, or settings text is read the way a narrative line is, adding each number it states and, with a scale, the scaled value too, so `$4.2M` records 4,200,000 and an ID such as `ORD-123` records nothing. A number written to d decimal places traces to a value within half a unit of its last place (`1.5e6` to within 50,000); a percent or percentage points also to a fraction 100 times smaller (`21%` to 0.214); a scale such as `k`, `M`, `bn`, or `million` also to the scaled value; and the lower bound of a range such as `10-15%` or `10 to 15%` takes the upper bound's unit. Scientific notation is read with a one-digit mantissa and an exponent of up to three digits, such as `1e-5`. Skipped as not quantities: code, HTML comments, link targets, numeric dates, times with or without am or pm, a day from 1 to 31 beside a month name that a date marks (before the month, as in `5 June`; after it with a year or punctuation following, as in `June 5, 2026` or `June 5.`; or after on, by, to, from, until, till, since, through, before, after, between, as of, or a weekday), a year from 1900 to 2099 after in, from, since, during, until, till, through, before, after, between, early, mid, late, fiscal, calendar, year, FY, CY, a month name, or a quarter or half such as Q3 or H1, or in parentheses, with any years listed after it (`between 2023 and 2024`), chart numbers and their lists (`Charts 1 and 4`), list markers, footnote marks, zero-padded codes such as release `002`, slash-joined numbers such as 3/4, and numbers glued to a word or name, such as Q2, Q-3, `chart-1`, `1st`, or a hash `12345e6`. Read, so reported unless they trace or are allowed: a number beside a month name without those marks (`In June 77 orders`, `June 5 we met`) and a year without that context (`1950 orders`, `2025 revenue`). Words such as three are not read. The check proves a number is among the recorded values, not that it is its claim's value: with a large represented table most small numbers trace.
- `verify` and `compare_trees` rows: `path`, `kind` (`missing`, `extra`, `size`, `checksum`), `expected`, and `actual`. `compare_trees(local_dir, copy_dir)` compares every file, manifest included, with the local release as expected. Invalid inventories, missing or unreadable directories, and symlinks raise errors, which the commands report under `errors` or `problem`.
