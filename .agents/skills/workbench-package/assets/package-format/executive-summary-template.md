# Executive summary

## Question and decision

State the business question, relevant scope, and the decision this work supports, or identify the exploratory purpose.

## Answer

Give the clearest evidence-supported answer in a short paragraph.

## Key findings

- Present the few findings that materially support or qualify the answer.
- Include important contrary evidence rather than smoothing it away.

## Implications

Explain what the findings mean for the decision and any evidence-supported next action.

## Confidence and caveats

Summarize the most decision-relevant source limitations, assumptions, unresolved issues, and findings awaiting revalidation. Keep each caveat consistent with the full journal.

## Package contents

Point readers to the analysis journal and list the selected supporting datasets, or state **None**.
