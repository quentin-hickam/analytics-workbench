### Added

- A review rule may name a scope, `- Scope: line`, `slide`, or `document`, set with `review-rules add --scope`: what one violation covers. `review-rules check` groups a `slide` rule's matches into one entry per slide, read as the section a `#`, `##`, or `###` heading opens as the file renders (one slide in `findings.md`, one section in `methodology.md` and the note), with its heading, heading line, and every matched line; a `document` rule's into one entry per file. An entry lists at most 20 lines, with `more_lines` beyond, and the row carries `scope`. A rule without the field is checked line by line, as before, and `add` writes the field only when given. A Scope other than exactly `line`, `slide`, or `document`, a blank one included, is a reported problem with the rule `unchecked`, and `add` refuses until it is repaired, as for other fields. `--scope` passes through the MCP `records` tool.
- The draft review checks claims of checking: every sentence in any narrative, the methodology note included, that asserts something was checked or verified, reconciles, sums or adds up to, or matches must cite the recorded result or check that establishes it and that covers all it asserts; `methodology.md` cites the result ID or evidence path, and `findings.md` and the note name the check in plain words, with its tolerance. A sentence without one is a narrative problem. It pairs with the warning `state finding` gives on the same wording.
- Within one `awb-package` request, a review run again after repairs may be a scoped re-review: given the prior report and a diff, the reviewer judges the changed lines, the prior report's violations and narrative problems, and every rule added since, and its last line opens `review (scoped):`. A full review is still required before the request's final `check-draft`, and `awb-release` always runs the full review.

### Changed

- The reviewer reports every violation and narrative problem in one pass, quoting each, and the caller settles them all, repairing each or, for a `warning`, reporting it for the user to accept, before the review runs again. A `slide` rule's violation is reported once per slide, at its heading line, and a `document` rule's once per file, quoting every offending line.
- A field line the rule parser does not accept is reported as "line is not a - Severity, Rule, Pattern, Scope, Fix, or Added field".
