### Added

- An optional audience-facing `methodology-note.md` in the package draft: a short plain-language account of method, sources, definitions, exclusions, and limitations for the brief's Audience, written from the new `package-format/methodology-note-template.md`, which `install_helpers.py --package-formats` installs. When the draft holds it, `check-draft`'s names, numbers, claims, and Markdown checks and `review-rules check` read it, with rows naming the file, and the claims check holds the whole note to the strongest represented claim strength. The draft review checks that it states nothing `methodology.md` does not support. It travels beside the deck like datasets and is not attached to the builder; `methodology.md` stays the internal reference. Without the file, both commands report exactly what they did before.
- A `## Appendix` deck section that holds `###` slides after the main sequence. **How we know**, **Limitations and open caveats**, **Supporting datasets**, and individual findings may move there, and a required slide moved there still counts. The outline reader marks appendix slides and keeps their order, every check reads them, and an appendix finding slide is held to its claim strength. `deck-rules.md` tells the builder to open the appendix with a divider slide titled Appendix, which the reference renderer builds. `check-draft` reports `slides`, the main sequence's and the appendix's slide counts.
- An optional manifest field, `presentation_order`: the finding slide headings in the order the user approved. When it is a list, `check-draft`'s `storyline` rows hold every finding slide to it and refuse a list that misses or repeats a finding slide, names a heading no finding slide has, or is malformed; `export` keeps it. The ledger-derived order stays the default proposal, and ledger priority and parent keep their analytical meaning, deciding Open questions membership.
- The investigation brief's Audience section records a deck length the user asks for and the headline checkpoint mode they prefer, and later packages reuse both. At the headline step, packaging reports the main and appendix slides against that length and proposes what moves to the appendix.

### Changed

- The headline checkpoint has two modes. **preview-and-wait** shows the title and every slide headline before the full slides are written; **draft-then-review** writes the full draft and shows its headlines, marked provisional, for approval or reorder. Draft-then-review is the default when the request supplies the storyline or asks for the full draft, and preview-and-wait otherwise. A consequential open choice, such as audience wording the user has not approved, still stops the draft in either mode and is asked in the same message. The headline step says in one sentence that the deck opens with a title slide and then the answer slide.
- `check-draft`'s `outline` check requires the title slide, **Answer** first after it, at least one finding slide in the main sequence, **How we know**, and **Limitations and open caveats**, once each and in that order, so a draft without one of them now fails. `findings-template.md` labels each section required, conditional, or optional, so the template and the check agree. Implications stays optional.
- An approved reorder of finding slides is recorded in `presentation_order` instead of keeping the ledger-derived order when priority and parent cannot express it.
- With **Limitations and open caveats** in the appendix, **Open questions** follows the last finding, Implications, or How we know slide of the main sequence.
